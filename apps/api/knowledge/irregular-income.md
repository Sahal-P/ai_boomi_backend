When money comes in on uneven days (daily wages, a crop sale, a shop's takings), plan for the hard months, not the good ones.

- **Budget on your lowest normal month.** Find a month when income was low but not unusual, and fit your essential spending inside it.
- **Keep a buffer.** In good months, put the extra into a separate "lean month" savings, not into new spending.
- **Pay yourself a "salary".** Keep income in one place and move a fixed weekly or monthly amount to the account you spend from.
- **Plan for the season.** If most income comes after harvest, divide it across the months until the next harvest, with school fees, seeds and festivals listed in advance.
- **Avoid using loans for regular expenses.** If a loan is needed for farming, prefer a bank, SHG or Kisan Credit Card over a moneylender.
