Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) is low-cost accident insurance through your bank account.

- **Who:** people aged 18 to 70 with a bank or post office account.
- **Cover:** ₹2 lakh for accidental death or total permanent disability, and ₹1 lakh for partial permanent disability.
- **Cost:** ₹20 a year (since June 2022), auto-debited from your account once a year.
- **Cover year:** June to May. Keep the balance in your account at renewal time so the cover continues.
- **Claims:** the nominee or the insured person applies through the bank with the required documents.

Join at your bank or through net banking. Check jansuraksha.gov.in for the current premium and rules.
