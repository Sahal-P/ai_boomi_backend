Small bank charges can quietly reduce your money every month.

- **Minimum balance:** many savings accounts charge a fee if the balance falls below a limit. A Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account (BSBDA), including Jan Dhan accounts, has no minimum balance.
- **ATM withdrawals:** banks allow a few free withdrawals a month; more can cost a fee. Plan withdrawals or use UPI.
- **SMS and card fees:** ask your bank which yearly fees apply and whether you need every card.
- **Cheque bounce and failed auto-debit charges** can be large. Keep enough balance before an EMI or auto-pay date.
- **Read the charges list:** every bank publishes its service charges. Ask the branch for them.

If a charge looks wrong, complain to your bank first. If it is not solved in 30 days, you can complain to the RBI Ombudsman at cms.rbi.org.in.
