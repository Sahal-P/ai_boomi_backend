Atal Pension Yojana (APY) is a government-backed pension scheme, mainly for workers without a pension at work.

- **Who can join:** Indian citizens aged 18 to 40 with a savings account. People who pay income tax cannot join (rule from October 2022).
- **What you get:** a guaranteed monthly pension from age 60, chosen between ₹1,000 and ₹5,000 a month. After the subscriber, the spouse gets the pension, and then the nominee gets the saved amount.
- **How much you pay:** a fixed monthly (or quarterly or half-yearly) amount, auto-debited from your account. It depends on your age when you join and the pension you choose: the younger you join, the less you pay.
- **Keep the balance** in your account on the debit date to avoid penalties.

Join at your bank branch or through your bank's app. Check current rules with your bank or on the PFRDA website.
