A self-help group (SHG) is a small group, often of women, that saves together and lends to its members. Many SHGs are part of the government's livelihood missions (such as DAY-NRLM, and Kudumbashree in Kerala) and get bank loans as a group.

- **Regular savings** by every member build the group's own fund.
- **Internal loans** from that fund are quick and usually cheaper than a moneylender.
- **Bank linkage:** groups with good records can get larger bank loans; some states offer interest support for women's SHGs.
- **Repay on time:** the group is responsible together, and good repayment opens bigger loans later.
- **Keep records:** note every saving and instalment. You can add SHG instalments as dues in FinBoy so it reminds you.

Ask your local SHG federation or block office how to join a group near you.
