For something big (a bike, a phone, a fridge), decide the target and the date first, then work backwards.

1. **Know the full price:** on-road price, insurance and accessories, not only the sticker price.
2. **Choose a date:** how many months until you want to buy.
3. **Save a fixed amount every month** towards it, kept apart from your emergency fund.
4. **Compare cash and EMI:** an EMI spreads the cost but adds interest and fees. Compare the total you will pay, not only the monthly amount. A bigger down payment means a smaller loan.
5. **Check it fits:** a new EMI should leave room for essentials and savings. Ask FinBoy whether a monthly amount fits your month.

Avoid "buy now, pay later" offers you have not compared, and never borrow from a loan app to make a down payment.
