Many instant loan apps are illegal. They charge huge hidden fees and harass borrowers and their contacts.

**Before borrowing from any app:**
- The lender must be a bank or an RBI-registered NBFC. The app should name it clearly; you can check NBFCs on the RBI website.
- You should get a Key Fact Statement with the full cost, fees and interest before signing.
- Be wary of apps that ask to read your contacts, photos or messages.
- Never pay a "processing fee" in advance to get a loan approved.

**If an app harasses you:**
- Do not pay into personal UPI IDs or keep taking new loans to repay old ones.
- Keep screenshots of the threats.
- Report the app on the RBI's Sachet portal (sachet.rbi.org.in) and report cybercrime on 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in. You can also go to the police.

A loan from a bank, an SHG or a cooperative is usually much cheaper and safer.
