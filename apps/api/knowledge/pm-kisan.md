PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi) gives income support of ₹6,000 a year to eligible farmer families, paid directly into their bank accounts in three instalments of ₹2,000.

- **Who:** landholding farmer families, with some exclusions, such as income-tax payers, institutional landholders, many government employees and certain professionals.
- **What you need:** your land records, an Aadhaar-linked bank account, and eKYC completed.
- **If an instalment did not come:** check your status on pmkisan.gov.in ("Know your status") and complete any pending eKYC or land-record verification.
- **Beware of fake messages** asking for money or OTPs to "release" your instalment. Use only the official website or your local agriculture office.

Rules can change. Check pmkisan.gov.in for the current eligibility and status.
