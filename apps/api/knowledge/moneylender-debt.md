Informal loans from moneylenders often charge very high interest, sometimes every week or month, which makes them hard to finish.

- **Know the real cost:** a small interest "per month" or "per week" adds up to a very large amount in a year.
- **Keep a written record** of every amount borrowed and repaid, with dates.
- **Look for cheaper options:** a bank loan, a self-help group (SHG) loan, a Kisan Credit Card for farming, or a loan from a regulated microfinance lender.
- **Use cheaper money to repay the costly loan first,** then stop borrowing from the moneylender.
- **Never let a loan roll over** by paying only the interest month after month.
- **Harassment is not allowed.** If someone threatens you, you can go to the police. Many states have laws that regulate moneylending.
