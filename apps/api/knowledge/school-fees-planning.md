School and college costs come at fixed times, so they can be planned.

- **List the whole year:** fees, books, uniforms, transport, exam fees and tuition.
- **Spread it out:** set aside a fixed amount each month so the big payments do not arrive all at once.
- **Mark the due dates:** add school fees as a due in FinBoy so it reminds you before the date.
- **Look for scholarships:** central and state scholarships are listed on the National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in). Check eligibility and deadlines every year.
- **For higher studies:** education loans from banks are usually cheaper than personal loans. Read the repayment terms before signing.

Saving for a daughter's education can also use the Sukanya Samriddhi scheme; ask FinBoy what it is.
