If a bank, card or UPI problem is not solved:

1. **Complain to the bank first:** at the branch, the official customer care number, or the bank's website or app. Keep the complaint number.
2. **Failed transactions:** for many failed UPI and card transactions, banks must reverse the money within set timelines. Note the transaction ID and date.
3. **If there is no reply in 30 days, or you are not satisfied,** complain to the RBI's Integrated Ombudsman online at cms.rbi.org.in. It covers banks, many NBFCs and payment systems. The service is free.
4. **For fraud,** report quickly on 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in as well.

Write down what happened, with dates, amounts and transaction IDs, before you complain. FinBoy's records can help you find the payment details.
