Diversification means spreading your savings across different kinds of places, so one bad event does not hurt all your money at once.

- **Different kinds of savings behave differently:** bank deposits are steady, shares and share-based funds rise and fall, and gold moves on its own.
- **Spreading reduces the risk of a big loss,** though it also means no single choice gives the very best result.
- **Start with the basics:** an emergency fund in a safe place, then insurance, then longer-term savings.
- **Match money to time:** money needed soon belongs in safe places; only money for many years ahead can take market ups and downs.
- **Do not chase tips** or put everything into one company, one scheme or one "sure" opportunity. Promises of very high, guaranteed returns are a warning sign.
