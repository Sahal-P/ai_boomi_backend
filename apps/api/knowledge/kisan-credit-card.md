A Kisan Credit Card gives farmers short-term credit for crops and related needs (seeds, fertiliser, labour) from banks, cooperatives and regional rural banks. It can also cover animal husbandry and fisheries.

- **Lower interest:** the government supports KCC loans with interest subvention, and farmers who repay on time get an extra incentive, so the effective rate is lower than most other loans, up to a limit. Ask your bank for the current rate and limit.
- **Flexible use:** draw money when needed during the season and repay after the harvest.
- **Repay on time** to keep the low rate and your eligibility for the next season.
- **How to apply:** at your bank branch with land records and identity documents. PM-KISAN beneficiaries can often apply more easily.

A KCC is usually much cheaper than borrowing from a moneylender for farm costs.
