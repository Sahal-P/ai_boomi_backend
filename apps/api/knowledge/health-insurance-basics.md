A hospital stay can wipe out years of savings. Health insurance pays for hospital treatment up to the cover amount.

- **Check free cover first:** your family may be eligible for Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) or your state's health scheme.
- **Family floater plans** share one cover amount across the family.
- **Read the limits:** waiting periods for existing illnesses, room-rent limits, co-payments, and which treatments are excluded.
- **Cashless network:** check which nearby hospitals are in the insurer's network.
- **Disclose honestly:** hiding an illness can lead to a rejected claim.
- **Keep your emergency fund** anyway; insurance may not cover everything.
