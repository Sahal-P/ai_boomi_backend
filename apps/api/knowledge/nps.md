The National Pension System (NPS) is a voluntary retirement savings account regulated by the PFRDA.

- **How it works:** you contribute regularly; the money is invested by regulated pension fund managers in a mix of shares and bonds, so the value goes up and down with markets.
- **Tier I** is the main retirement account, with limited withdrawals before 60. **Tier II** is an optional, more flexible account.
- **At retirement,** part of the money can be taken out and part must be used to buy a pension (an annuity) that pays monthly. Check the current withdrawal rules, which can change.
- **Tax benefits** are available on contributions under the rules that apply to you.

NPS suits people who want long-term retirement savings and are fine with market ups and downs. For a guaranteed small pension, see the Atal Pension Yojana. FinBoy does not choose fund managers or plans for you.
