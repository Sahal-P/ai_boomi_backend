A credit score is a number (usually 300 to 900) that credit bureaus calculate from your loan and credit card history. Lenders use it to decide whether to lend and at what rate. CIBIL is one bureau; Experian, Equifax and CRIF High Mark are others.

**What helps your score:**
- Paying every EMI and credit card bill on time, in full if possible.
- Using only a part of your credit card limit.
- Not applying for many loans or cards in a short time.
- Keeping old accounts that are in good standing.

**What hurts it:** late or missed payments, settled or written-off loans, and many recent loan enquiries.

You can get a free credit report from each bureau once a year. Check it for mistakes, such as loans you never took, and ask the bureau to correct them.
