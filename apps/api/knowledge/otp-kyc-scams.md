Common tricks to steal money:

- **"Your KYC has expired, your account will be blocked"** messages with a link. Banks do not block accounts by SMS links. Update KYC only at your branch or in the bank's official app.
- **Calls asking for an OTP, PIN, CVV or password.** Banks, RBI, police and government offices never ask for these. Hang up.
- **"Install this app so I can help you"** (screen-sharing apps). With screen sharing, the caller can see your OTPs and passwords. Never install apps on a stranger's request.
- **Prizes, lotteries, jobs or refunds** that need a "small fee" first.
- **Fake customer care numbers** found on search results. Use only numbers from the bank's official website or app.

If you shared details or lost money, call your bank to block the account or card, then call 1930 or report on cybercrime.gov.in quickly.
