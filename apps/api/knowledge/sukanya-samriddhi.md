Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana is a government savings scheme for a girl child, opened at a post office or authorised bank.

- **Who:** a parent or guardian can open it for a daughter below 10 years of age.
- **Deposits:** at least ₹250 and at most ₹1.5 lakh in a financial year, for 15 years from opening.
- **Maturity:** the account matures 21 years after it is opened. Part can be withdrawn for higher education after the girl turns 18.
- **Interest:** set by the government every quarter and usually higher than a savings account. Check the current rate before opening.
- **Missing a year** keeps the account inactive until a small penalty and the minimum deposit are paid.

It suits long-term saving for a daughter's education and future. Check the current rules with your post office or on the National Savings Institute website.
