A budget is a plan for your money before the month starts, so that money goes where you choose.

1. **Write down what comes in** each month: salary, wages, crop or milk sales, money from family.
2. **List the fixed payments first**: rent, EMIs, school fees, electricity, phone recharge.
3. **Set aside savings next**, even a small amount, before spending on anything else.
4. **Give a limit to everyday spending**: food, groceries, transport, small treats.
5. **Check every week**. FinBoy's "this week" and "this month" check-ups and budget warnings help.

A common starting rule is to keep needs first, then savings, then wants. If needs take almost all of your income, start with a very small saving and look for one cost you can cut.

If a month goes over the limit, do not give up. Move money from a lower-priority limit, and set the next month's limits a little more realistically.
