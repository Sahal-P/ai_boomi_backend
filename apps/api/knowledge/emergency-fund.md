An emergency fund is money kept aside only for real emergencies: illness, a job or crop loss, an urgent repair. It stops one bad month from turning into a loan.

- **How much:** a common goal is three to six months of your essential expenses (rent, food, school fees, EMIs). Start with one month; even a small amount helps.
- **Where:** somewhere safe and easy to take out quickly, such as a savings account or a recurring deposit, kept apart from daily spending money.
- **How to build it:** set a fixed amount every month, or put away a part of every good month. Add bonuses or extra income to it.
- **When to use it:** only for real emergencies, not for planned purchases or festivals. If you use it, refill it first before other savings goals.

Build the emergency fund before taking risks with investments. With it, a hospital visit or a bad month does not force you to borrow at high interest.
