Life insurance protects people who depend on your income if you die.

- **Protection first:** if your family depends on your income, the main need is enough cover. A pure term plan gives a large cover for a relatively small premium but pays nothing if you survive the term.
- **Mixed plans** that combine insurance and saving usually give much less cover for the same premium. Understand what you are buying.
- **Low-cost options:** PMJJBY gives ₹2 lakh life cover through your bank account for a small yearly premium.
- **Buy from IRDAI-registered insurers,** read the policy document, and keep nominees up to date.
- **Never buy under pressure,** and be careful of calls promising bonuses or refunds on old policies; these are often frauds.
