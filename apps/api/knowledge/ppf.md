The Public Provident Fund (PPF) is a government-backed long-term savings account at a post office or bank.

- **Term:** 15 years, which can be extended in blocks of 5 years.
- **Deposits:** at least ₹500 and at most ₹1.5 lakh in a financial year, in one or more instalments.
- **Interest:** set by the government every quarter; your money is safe because the government backs it.
- **Access:** limited. Partial withdrawals are allowed after some years, and loans against the balance in the early years. Do not use it for money you may need soon.
- **Tax:** deposits, interest and maturity have tax benefits under the old tax regime.

It suits steady, safe saving for long goals such as retirement. Keep your emergency fund separately. Check current rates with your bank or post office.
