When there are several loans, a clear order helps you finish them faster.

1. **List every loan:** who it is from, the amount left, the monthly payment and the interest rate.
2. **Always pay the minimum on every loan** so you do not add penalties and late fees.
3. **Put any extra money on one loan at a time:**
   - the one with the **highest interest** first saves the most money, or
   - the **smallest** first gives a quick win that keeps you going.
4. **When one is finished,** add its payment to the next one.
5. **Do not take a new loan to pay an old one** unless it is clearly cheaper and from a regulated lender.
6. **Talk to the lender** early if you cannot pay. Banks can sometimes reschedule. If a lender harasses you, complain to the bank and, for regulated lenders, to the RBI (cms.rbi.org.in).
