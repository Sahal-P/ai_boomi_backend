An EMI (equated monthly instalment) is the fixed amount you pay every month on a loan. Each EMI pays some interest and some of the loan itself.

- **Early EMIs are mostly interest.** The loan amount goes down slowly at first and faster later.
- **Total cost = EMI × number of months.** Compare loans by the total you will pay, including processing fees, not only by the EMI.
- **A longer loan means a smaller EMI but more interest** in total.
- **Prepaying** (paying extra when you can) reduces the interest you pay. Ask whether there is a prepayment charge.
- **Fixed or floating:** a floating-rate EMI or tenure can change when interest rates change.
- **"No-cost EMI"** offers can still include fees or a hidden discount loss. Read the terms.
- **Missing an EMI** adds penalties and hurts your credit score.

Before taking a loan, ask FinBoy whether the monthly amount fits your month.
