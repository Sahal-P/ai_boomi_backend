Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) lets anyone open a basic savings bank account with no minimum balance.

- **Zero balance:** no charge for keeping a low balance.
- **RuPay debit card** with accident insurance cover (₹2 lakh for accounts opened after August 2018).
- **Overdraft facility:** eligible account holders with a good record can get a small overdraft.
- **Direct benefit transfers:** government payments such as subsidies and scheme money can come straight into the account.
- **Gateway to other schemes:** life and accident insurance (PMJJBY, PMSBY) and the Atal Pension Yojana can be added through the account.

Open one at any bank branch or business correspondent (bank mitra) with your Aadhaar or another valid identity document. Check pmjdy.gov.in for current features.
