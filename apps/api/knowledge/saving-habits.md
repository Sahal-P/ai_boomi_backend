Saving is easier when it happens before spending, not after.

- **Save first:** on the day money comes in, move your saving amount out first. What is left is for the month.
- **Make it automatic:** a recurring deposit or a standing instruction moves money without you having to decide each time.
- **Start small:** a small amount every day adds up over a month. Raise it when your income grows.
- **Keep it separate:** money kept in a different account or envelope is spent less.
- **Watch small leaks:** small daily payments (tea, snacks, recharges, subscriptions) add up. FinBoy's report of small payments shows how much.
- **Give savings a name:** "school fees", "bike", "emergency". A named goal is easier to keep.
