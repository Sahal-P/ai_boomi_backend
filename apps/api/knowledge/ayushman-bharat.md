Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) gives eligible families health cover of up to ₹5 lakh per family per year for hospital treatment at empanelled government and private hospitals.

- **Who:** families that meet the scheme's eligibility (based on government data, with state expansions). Since 2024, people aged 70 and above are covered regardless of income.
- **Cashless:** treatment at empanelled hospitals is cashless and paperless; show your Ayushman card.
- **Check eligibility and get the card:** on beneficiary.nha.gov.in, the Ayushman app, or at a Common Service Centre or empanelled hospital help desk.
- **Many states run their own schemes** alongside it (for example Kerala's KASP); ask at the hospital's help desk.

Rules change; check pmjay.gov.in for current eligibility. For families not covered, a private health policy may still be worth considering.
