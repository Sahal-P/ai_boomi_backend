A SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) is a way to put a fixed amount into a mutual fund every month, automatically.

- **It is not a product itself;** it is a method of investing regularly. The returns depend on the fund and the market.
- **Market risk:** the value can go down as well as up, especially over short periods. Returns are not guaranteed.
- **Time matters:** regular investing over many years smooths out the ups and downs; money needed within a year or two is better kept in safer options.
- **Before starting:** build an emergency fund, clear high-interest debt, and make sure the monthly amount fits your budget (FinBoy can check that).
- **Choosing a fund:** FinBoy does not recommend funds. For personal advice, consult a SEBI-registered investment adviser, and read the fund's documents.

Investor education is available from SEBI (investor.sebi.gov.in) and AMFI.
