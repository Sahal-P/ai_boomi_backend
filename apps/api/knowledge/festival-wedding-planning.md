Festivals and weddings are known in advance, so they can be saved for instead of borrowed for.

- **List the events of the year** and a rough amount for each: clothes, gifts, travel, food.
- **Save a little every month** in a separate "events" fund, so the money is ready when the day comes.
- **Set a limit before shopping** and keep to it. Decide the gift and clothes budget per person.
- **For a wedding, plan early:** the biggest costs (venue, food, jewellery) can be fixed first, and the rest fitted around them.
- **Avoid high-interest loans:** a moneylender or a loan app can turn one wedding into years of debt. If a loan is needed, compare banks and SHGs and borrow only what you can repay.
