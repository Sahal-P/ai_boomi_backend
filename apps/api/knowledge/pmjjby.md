Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY) is low-cost life insurance through your bank account.

- **Who:** people aged 18 to 50 with a bank or post office account; cover can continue up to age 55.
- **Cover:** ₹2 lakh paid to the nominee if the insured person dies from any cause.
- **Cost:** ₹436 a year (since June 2022), auto-debited once a year; cover runs June to May.
- **Starting rule:** when you first join, there is a short waiting period for deaths other than by accident.
- **Keep your account funded** at renewal so the cover does not lapse, and keep your nominee up to date.

It is a simple, cheap base cover for a family that depends on your income. Check jansuraksha.gov.in for the current premium and rules.
