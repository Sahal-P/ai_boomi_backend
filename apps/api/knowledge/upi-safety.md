UPI is safe when a few rules are kept:

- **Your UPI PIN is only for sending money.** You never need it to receive money. Anyone who asks you to enter your PIN "to receive" is cheating you.
- **Check before you approve a request.** A "collect request" takes money from you if you approve it. Decline requests you do not recognise.
- **Scanning a QR code sends money.** You do not scan a code to receive money.
- **Never share your PIN or OTP** with anyone, including people who say they are from your bank, UPI app or a government office.
- **Check the name** shown before paying.
- **If you are cheated,** call 1930 (the national cybercrime helpline) or report on cybercrime.gov.in at once, and tell your bank. Quick reports give the best chance of stopping the money.
- **Money sent to a wrong number:** contact your bank and UPI app's support with the transaction ID.
